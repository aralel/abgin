Privacy policy

Version: 22 November 2026

Who is responsible

Aralel GmbH, Josefstr. 66, 52080 Aachen, Germany. Email: privacy@aralel.com.

The app: no account, no analytics

Abgin for iPhone and Android has no user accounts and no analytics. We do not ask for your name, email or location.

Your progress

Stars, solved levels, the daily streak and your settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Abgin, Apple keeps a copy in your iCloud account so another device can continue your progress. On Android, Android Auto Backup keeps a copy of this file in your Google account backup, if backup is turned on, so a reinstall or a new phone keeps your progress. We never receive this data.

Game Center (iPhone)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Abgin sends two scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards: your total level stars and your daily streak. Apple shows them, with your Game Center nickname, to other players and shows you your rank. The app keeps your Game Center player ID with your progress so scores are only sent for the account the progress belongs to. We never receive your Game Center data; Apple processes it under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, as the leaderboard is a feature you choose by signing in. You can sign out of Game Center, or hide yourself from leaderboards, in the iOS Settings app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Abgin shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks for your choices where the law requires it. Depending on your choices, Google may process your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt; on Android the advertising ID, through the AD_ID permission), IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), or for non-personalised ads our legitimate interest in funding the free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Error reports (Sentry)

When the app crashes or an error occurs, a report is sent to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.; data stored in the EU). It contains technical information only: app version, device model, iOS or Android version and where in the code the error happened. It contains no personal details and is not used for advertising. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Reports are deleted after 90 days.

Play statistics

When the app starts and when you solve a level, the app sends a short message to our own server (relay.codehospital.com, operated for Aralel GmbH): the device model, the app version and, for a solve, the level, the stars and the number of moves. It contains no identifier and is not linked to you or used for advertising. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the levels (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Google Play

On Android, Abgin is installed and updated through Google Play (Google Ireland Limited). When the app checks for updates, Google Play handles this under Google's privacy policy.

Beta testing (TestFlight)

If you join the iPhone beta, Apple runs it through TestFlight under Apple's privacy policy. Apple shares with us the crash reports, usage statistics such as sessions and installs, and any feedback and screenshots you choose to send from TestFlight, together with the name and email address you used if you joined by email invitation. We use this only to fix and improve the beta, and delete it when the beta ends. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR. You can leave the beta at any time in the TestFlight app.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no content from other servers (no external fonts, scripts or embeds). On Android phones and iPhones it shows a prompt to install the app; if you close it, your browser's local storage remembers the time for 14 days so the prompt stays away. This value never leaves your device and you can delete it with your browser's site data. When you visit it, our hosting provider GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (GitHub Pages) processes your IP address, the time and the requested page in server logs, to deliver the site and keep it secure. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub keeps these logs under its own privacy statement; we do not receive them. GitHub is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer to the USA rests on an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing and to data portability (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to a supervisory authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen. Contact us at privacy@aralel.com.

Changes

We update this policy when the app or the website changes.
