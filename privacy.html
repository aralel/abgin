Privacy policy

Version: 18 November 2026

Who is responsible

Aralel GmbH, Josefstr. 66, 52080 Aachen, Germany. Email: privacy@aralel.com.

The app: no account, no analytics

Abgin has no user accounts and no analytics. We do not ask for your name, email or location.

Your progress

Stars, solved levels, the daily streak and your settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Abgin, Apple keeps a copy in your iCloud account so another device can continue your progress. We never receive this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Abgin shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks for your choices where the law requires it. Depending on your choices, Google may process your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt), IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), or for non-personalised ads our legitimate interest in funding the free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Error reports (Sentry)

When the app crashes or an error occurs, a report is sent to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.; data stored in the EU). It contains technical information only: app version, device model, iOS version and where in the code the error happened. It contains no personal details and is not used for advertising. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Reports are deleted after 90 days.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no content from other servers (no external fonts, scripts or embeds). When you visit it, our hosting provider GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (GitHub Pages) processes your IP address, the time and the requested page in server logs, to deliver the site and keep it secure. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub keeps these logs under its own privacy statement; we do not receive them. GitHub is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer to the USA rests on an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing and to data portability (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to a supervisory authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen. Contact us at privacy@aralel.com.

Changes

We update this policy when the app or the website changes.
