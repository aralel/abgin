Questions and answers

What is Abgin?

A calm puzzle game for iPhone. Every swipe moves two pieces: an amber circle on the top board and its reflection, a teal diamond, on the board below the water. Up and down move both the same way, left and right are mirrored. You win when both reach their rings on the same move. How to play

Is Abgin free?

Yes. Abgin is free to download and play, with a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no currencies.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Can I play it before release?

Yes. Join the public beta on TestFlight: install Apple's free TestFlight app, open the link on your iPhone and tap Accept. Beta builds expire after 90 days; TestFlight offers each new one. Feedback and screenshots you send from TestFlight reach us directly.

Is every level solvable?

Yes. Every level is checked by a solver that tries every possible sequence of swipes. The solver also sets each level's par: the fewest moves that solve it.

I'm stuck. What can I do?

Undo as many moves as you like, or restart; a restart can be undone too. For a nudge, tap the light bulb: after a short ad, an arrow shows the best next move. If no ad is available, the hint is free.

How do stars work?

Solve a level in par moves for three stars, within about a third more moves for two, and any solve earns one. A hint does not cost you stars.

When do the next chapters open?
  • Still water: solve 12 levels of First light.
  • Frost: solve 20 levels of Still water.
  • Locks: earn 120 stars.
  • Twin glass: earn 180 stars.

Inside a chapter, the next three unsolved levels are always open, so one hard level never blocks you.

How does the daily puzzle work?

After 12 levels of First light, a new puzzle appears every day at midnight, your local time. Solve one each day to build a streak. If you miss one or two days, you can keep your streak for 5 stars; after three missed days it starts again.

Who made this level?

Every level shows its creator while you play, like "by Aralel".

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if iCloud is turned on for Abgin. Your stars, solved levels and streak sync through your own iCloud account. Progress from two devices is combined, keeping the best result for each level.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection. Ads need one; without an ad, hints are free.

Does Abgin collect my data?

There is no account and no analytics. Ads come from Google AdMob: where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choices first, and you can change them any time in Settings → Privacy options. If the app crashes, a technical error report without personal details helps us fix it. Details in the privacy policy.

Is Abgin accessible?

The game supports VoiceOver (move with the four direction actions instead of swiping), Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion. The two pieces have different shapes, not only different colours, and doors and switches are paired by letter.

Can I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Yes, in Settings. Abgin also follows the silent switch and plays alongside your own music.
