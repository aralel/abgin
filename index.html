One swipe,
two worlds.

Abgin is a calm puzzle game for iPhone. Every swipe moves a piece and its reflection at the same time: up and down together, left and right mirrored. Get both home on the same move.

Coming soon to the App Store How to play

Free on iPhone with iOS 18 or later. No account, no in-app purchases.

A small idea that keeps unfolding

Two boards, one above the water and one below. The pieces always move together, but walls stop only the piece that hits them. That is where the puzzles begin.

Two boards, one swipe

Up and down move both pieces the same way. Left and right are mirrored. Simple to learn, surprisingly deep.

Five chapters

From small 4×4 boards to 8×8, each chapter adds one new idea: walls, pits, ice, switches and doors, swap tiles.

A new puzzle every day

A fresh daily puzzle at midnight. Build a streak, and if life gets in the way, keep it for a few stars.

Every level is solvable

Each level is proven solvable by a solver, which also sets its par. Solve it in par moves for three stars.

Never stuck

Unlimited undo and restart. If you want help, watch a short ad to see the best next move.

Calm by design

No timers, no lives, no pressure. Soft water, gentle sound and haptics, and support for VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion.

Learn it in one minute

Swipe anywhere. The amber circle moves on the top board, the teal diamond on the bottom board. Bring both to their rings on the same move.

Questions

Is Abgin free?

Yes. Abgin is free to download and play. A small banner ad at the bottom of the screen keeps it free, and there are no in-app purchases.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Does Abgin collect my data?

There is no account and no analytics. Ads come from Google, only with your consent where the law requires it, and you can change your choices in Settings. Read the privacy policy.
